# Continue a held predictive learned review

A learned effect with an originally required pre-output predictor can retain its
native or fixed process review after `HoldEffect` and receive an explicitly
requested successor. This covers [fixed native continuation](LEARNED_HELD_REVIEW.md),
the [prepaid native reserve](LEARNED_RESERVED_REVIEW.md) and
[original fixed process continuation](PROCESS_LEARNED_HELD_REVIEW.md). The original action,
learned sidecar, evaluator owners, numerical work and forecast observation remain
in the same live owner.

The contract implements the composition of plan §9.8 with the pre-action
consistency residual in §12.7 and its lifetime evidence in §13.2. It serves
FI-A06/FI-A09/FI-A12/FI-A13/FI-A16. A quiet prediction is an observation; fresh
helper review and the independent human publication key remain separate gates.

## Match the original predictor contract

Learned continuation distinguishes three admitted configurations:

- An ordinary generation has neither a declared predictor nor an installed
  consistency lane.
- A required raw pre-output recipe matches the exact installed consistency
  configuration and original hosted residual source, with no learned predictor
  substituted into that lane.
- A required owned pre-output recipe matches the exact installed learned
  predictor and its underlying consistency configuration, using the original
  owned generation mode.

An optional observer, an undeclared predictor, a mismatched installed
configuration or a change of source mode cannot borrow one of these profiles.
The actual live native or process wrapper and its unused preprovisioned
reviewers remain required, with distinct private ticket provenance. A generic
file/socket launcher, historical receipt or copied packet cannot reconstruct
either owner. A process successor additionally requires confirmed reaping of
every predecessor direct child before source or clock acquisition.

This is a review continuation, so it does not require the distinct owned
prompt-audit reset selection. It performs no checkpoint restore or predictor
restart and grants no new forecast budget.

## Retain the exact settled observation

The original request must already have consumed its own forecast when its
generated action was observed. Every current held-review offer and ticket
requires the same native consistency law used for publication:

- No pending forecast or pending request-to-forecast binding remains.
- Prediction coverage has not been lost, and the lifetime evidence has not
  crossed its registered threshold.
- The observation belongs to this attempt, its actor revision and authority
  epoch are current, and the predictor still matches the current policy
  generation.

The source-bound sidecar, input revision, complete action and confirmed audience
must also remain current. A Message observation cannot stand in for the
separately forecast Finish, or for another generated Message.

The checks occur before a held offer or ticket can authorize acquisition. The
same checks run again after source and clock acquisition, immediately before
the successor's original Begin. Original review application and final publication
retain their existing consistency gates. Lost, pending, crossed or stale evidence
therefore cannot open another review merely because an earlier Hold succeeded.

An already settled forecast is not evaluated again against its former pending
expiry. That expiry governed the original observation. The held action's
unchanged deadline, current policy/authority/actor and successor window still
bound continuation. This neither renews an expired pending forecast nor turns
an unanswered forecast into evidence.

## Preserve finite work and independent approval

Either fixed mode consumes only its next original round ID, evaluator cohort
and window. The native reserve consumes the next exact cohort paid for before
Begin and admits an unused round ID with a host-selected window inside the
original action deadline. Each owner retains its cumulative reservations, polls,
records and earlier review history. Process continuation spends one original
protocol poll for launch and cannot borrow native reserve capacity.

Re-review generates no actor token, reruns no predictor and adds no likelihood
sample. The original forecast observation, prediction spending and lifetime
evidence remain intact through Hold, successor admission, review and human
approval or rejection. Fixing a source file or reconnecting cannot replenish
helper capacity, predictor allowance or operator grant.

Current source acquisition, original topology custody, Stop, cancellation and
the complete guarded publication requirements still apply. An admitted failure
keeps its spent operation and work; it cannot replace a reviewer after seeing
its answer. Only a new original Continue can advance the original effect to the
separate current human decision and final publication checks.

## Use the existing live operator workflow

The [live learned operator](LIVE_LEARNED_OPERATOR.md#continue-a-held-learned-review)
and [resident learned service](RESIDENT_LEARNED_PUBLICATION.md#an-explicitly-selected-successor-after-hold)
accept the existing finite `convening` selection for original native or fixed
process recipes with required raw or owned pre-output prediction. Select this grant before the
first review; no new command flag, wire schema or authority is introduced.

After an applied Hold without automatic refinement, send authenticated Tower
`next` to the original request with `action: "convene_held"`. A progressive
native or process recipe selects its exact next fixed round; a one-round native
recipe with a funded reserve selects an unused round ID. Inspect the returned action and explicitly
submit its `convene` document. A Pending response means admission; Opened
requires the retained owner's fresh source checks and successful durable Begin.

The original wrapper remains live throughout the wait, with independent Stop,
actor cancellation, cumulative transport and original helper work, and the unchanged
action deadline. Successful re-review proceeds to a fresh independent human
Approve/Reject decision. Operator grants cannot fund a missing evaluator
cohort, extend predictor work, authorize publication or recover a historical
held owner. Every selected resident recipe must retain exactly one original
evaluator family, and the grant remains finite across releases and reconnects.

## Qualification

Native lifecycle, causal-negative and actual operator-command regression
sources accompany this implementation. Synthetic actor and helper models exercise protocol behavior;
they do not establish predictor calibration or detector reliability. Scoped
source review and syntax parsing are distinct from the required RCH compiler,
runtime, formatting, Clippy and full qualification gates. Execution status is
recorded in `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`; no gate is closed by this contract.

The command regressions cover raw/owned prediction with independent human
approval and rejection, retained original helper and forecast inputs, source
loss, Stop and native capacity exhaustion. Their execution remains subject to
the same pending RCH qualification.

The fixed-process service regression covers both predictor modes with fresh
human approval and rejection, original settled work and byte-identical sidecars.
Those process cases have passed source review and scoped syntax parsing only;
their compilation and runtime execution remain unqualified.
