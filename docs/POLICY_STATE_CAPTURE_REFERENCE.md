# Captured policy-state streams

Consumer: the existing effect policy's `Snapshot` input. This implements a reference observation-source slice of FA-017 / FA-063 and the read-witness requirements of FA-058, serving the behavioral observation and complete-evidence requirements in plan sections 7, 14 and 17.1. It adds no runtime, database, credentials or cryptographic claim.

`PolicyStateCapture` and its separately held noncloneable `PolicyStateWriter` bind one source generation and complete action scope. The writer is provisioned to a trusted observation adapter, not to an actor or helper. Its observations, completeness assertions and semantic epochs still require authentication and a correct physical source outside this module. The finite domain is exactly the supplied policy-state map, not the entire external world.

The writer records sequence-numbered full images and compare-and-update batches. Out-of-order arrivals remain retained, but a missing middle or any unclosed tail makes the live capture unavailable. All delta preimages are checked against one predecessor and all edits are applied together; cardinality and byte limits apply to the resulting state rather than a transient insertion order. A full image is required before deltas and whenever semantics change. Older semantic epochs cannot be restored.

A separately numbered closure marker must cover the entire observed, contiguous prefix. A new frame immediately withdraws the previous live closure. An exact duplicate is idempotent; a conflicting duplicate, invalid new frame or dropped observation due to capacity latches a source fault. Such an error deliberately does not preserve the old snapshot as eligible. Withdrawal without a fault requires a newly observed full image after the withdrawal cut. A new marker over old records cannot heal lost observation. Dropping the writer disables live capture; retained immutable snapshots remain historical evidence only.

The limits are 1,024 lifetime frames, a 64-sequence reorder window, 256 keys, 8 KiB per value, 64 KiB per complete snapshot, 64 changes per transaction and 8 MiB of retained event value/preimage bytes. Fixed metadata, allocator overhead and repeated transient map copies are not included in the logical-byte counter. Every retained event is readable through the standalone capture for bounded replay/investigation. This is not persistent storage, throughput qualification or independently authenticated stream closure.

A `CapturedSnapshot` shares an immutable snapshot and its exact source/sequence/closure marker. `validate` compares every supplied snapshot field against the current eligible capture. Historical capture is neither permission nor a substitute for a live recheck. The producer cannot manufacture an effect permit or a congress result.

## Original broker integration

`DeliveryBroker::enable_policy_state` binds the capture before any proposal or control transition and returns the only writer to the trusted observation adapter. The same API is forwarded by `OversightBroker`, so it is available to the original actor supervisor and supervised driver without a second controller. There is no disable operation, mutable capture accessor or one-key/two-key bypass.

The delivery owner checks its current complete capture at proposal, review creation, permissive review application, authorization and the shared final dispatch implementation. Every field of the supplied Snapshot must equal that capture, including completeness and semantic epoch. It then runs the unchanged exact policy and judgment-witness checks. An unrelated closed state update can reuse a still-valid original permit with the fresh complete Snapshot; an unclosed update or replayed old Snapshot cannot. A relevant state mutation also fails the original policy/witness check even when the supplied Snapshot correctly reflects the new capture.

The exact immutable captured Snapshot and frontier are retained with each dispatched record, outside the endpoint request projection. Later updates cannot rewrite the consumed basis or expose policy-state bytes to the actor or publication endpoint. At most 128 delivery records retain at most 64 KiB of state values per distinct cut, in addition to the existing action and event bounds. Equal cuts share an Rc. This is bounded logical retention, not measured heap cost or inclusion in the payload-only action-byte counter.

`replace_policy_state` is a trusted source-generation transition with exact expected source generation and authority epoch. It preserves source identity and full action scope, advances the original authority revocation floor, cancels every undispatched attempt and refunds only its reservation. The new source starts unavailable and requires its own full image and closure. Old writer handles affect only their retired source. The last applied semantic epoch is retained as a minimum across replacement, so repairing source connectivity cannot silently reinterpret data under an older semantic epoch. At most 16 source generations are admitted over this broker's lifetime.

Source loss, poisoning and replacement never imply that an already admitted effect failed to execute. Previously completed restrictive reviews can still be applied without a currently eligible source; cancellation, terminal receipt acceptance and outstanding-effect reconciliation remain available. No source operation refunds an unknown effect or resends a publication. Endpoint fencing, external-outcome durability and the host's observation-authenticity/freshness contract remain separate. In particular, a closed prefix is not a measurement of unobserved external changes, and the capture adds no wall-clock freshness lease or independent host monitor.

## Verification boundary

September 11, 2026, first increment: added stream reconstruction, atomic deltas, closure/loss semantics and nine public regression functions, including all 24 arrival permutations of four dependent updates. They cover honest empty domains, forged complete flags, equivocation, semantic rollback, bounds, missing tails, full-image recovery and writer loss.

Second increment: integrated the source with the original broker and added seven public dispatch/regeneration/reconciliation functions. They pair one-key and two-key publication with an unrelated unclosed update, refuse stale whole snapshots independently of policy-key invalidation, preserve a single reservation on relevant-state refusal, fence old attempts during repair, preserve consumed capture and executed outcomes after writer loss, and keep restrictive review application available. The tests also check foreign/stale replacement atomicity and the cross-generation semantic floor.

The required Cargo/rustc/rustfmt/RCH tools are unavailable here; these tests and the compile-fail example have not been compiled or executed. No production or Beads qualification is claimed. Existing qualified receipts remain historical. Stream reconstruction and broker integration are implemented source, not evidence of physical observation coverage, authenticated provider capture, persistent authority, production runtime admission or an executed release gate.
