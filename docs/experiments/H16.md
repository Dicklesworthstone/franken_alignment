# H16 — Elicited Signatures Transfer and Honeypots Stay Hidden

- **Hypothesis ID:** `H16`
- **Founding Roots:** `FI-A10`, `FI-A11`
- **Plan Sections:** `§12.2`, `§12.3`, `§19`, `§20`
- **Preregistration Status:** `preregistered`

## Question

Do activation signatures learned from controlled elicitation campaigns transfer to detect held-out harmful mechanisms and prompt families at lower cost than text/tool monitors alone, while honeypot tasks remain statistically indistinguishable from real work using only the actor's observable view? A positive result applies only to the evaluated mechanism holdouts and actor view channels; it does not prove transfer to arbitrary unseen capabilities.

## Treatments

1. **Text/Tool Monitor Baseline (`T0_text_tool`):** Surface transcript and tool-call monitor operating at matched compute and byte budget.
2. **Concept-Anchor Similarity Only (`T1_anchor_only`):** Cosine/Mahalanobis distance to static known-bad concept anchors without elicitation trajectories.
3. **Single-Mechanism Elicited Signatures (`T2_single_mech`):** Activation signatures fit on N-1 harmful mechanisms, holding out the target mechanism family.
4. **Multi-Mechanism Elicited Signatures (`T3_multi_mech`):** Joint signature library fit across diverse elicitation scenarios outside the held-out prompt and mechanism lineage.
5. **Combined Activation + Surface Monitor (`T4_combined`):** Multi-mechanism activation signature library fused with the surface monitor under the same total budget.

## Outcomes

- **Primary Outcomes:**
  1. **Transfer Recall at Fixed FPR:** True-positive detection rate on held-out harmful mechanisms and prompt lineages at fixed benign false-positive rates ($\text{FPR} \in \{0.01, 0.005\}$).
  2. **Honeypot Indistinguishability (`AUC_disc`):** Area under the ROC curve of a trained adversarial discriminator attempting to classify honeypot versus real tasks from the actor's exact input view (prompts, tool responses, clock/latency metadata).
- **Unconditional Denominator:** All evaluated held-out benign, real-work, honeypot, and harmful trials ($N_{\text{total}}$).
- **Attack-Conditioned Denominator:** All held-out non-lineage harmful-mechanism trials ($N_{\text{heldout\_attack}}$).
- **Countermetrics:**
  - False-positive hold rate on vocabulary-matched benign tasks containing security/oversight keywords.
  - Discrimination accuracy (`AUC_disc`) when timing metadata is included versus masked.
  - Per-session probe evaluation bytes and latency overhead.

## Controls

- **Contaminated-Lineage Control:** Evaluation on in-lineage elicitation prompts is recorded in a separate diagnostic column and strictly excluded from headline transfer metrics.
- **Vocabulary-Matched Benign Control:** Benign tasks matching the token distribution and tool-call schemas of the harmful tasks.
- **Sham Anchor Control:** Signatures fit on random activation directions to bound chance detection.
- **Timing/Tool Fidelity Audit:** Independent check that honeypot tool adapters return byte-identical error schemas and latency envelopes to real adapters.

## Sample Size

- Minimum $N_{\text{heldout\_attack}} \ge 400$ held-out harmful trials across at least 4 disjoint mechanism families, $N_{\text{benign}} \ge 1,200$ vocabulary-matched benign trials, and $N_{\text{honeypot\_pair}} \ge 600$ paired honeypot/real tasks for discriminator evaluation.

## Confidence Method

- Stratified bootstrap (10,000 resamples by mechanism family and task template) for recall at fixed FPR and `AUC_disc` 95% confidence intervals.
- Exact Clopper-Pearson binomial bounds for per-stratum false-positive rates.
- DeLong test and bootstrap percentile intervals for `AUC_disc - 0.50`.

## Kill Criterion

The hypothesis is falsified if either:
1. Out-of-lineage recall of `T3_multi_mech` at $\text{FPR} = 0.01$ fails to exceed `T0_text_tool` by at least 0.05 (or collapses by $> 50\%$ relative to in-lineage recall, indicating prompt memorization); OR
2. The actor-view discriminator achieves an upper 95% confidence bound on `AUC_disc > 0.55` on any honeypot stratum. Any distinguishable honeypot stratum must be immediately withdrawn rather than iteratively tuned against the discriminator.
