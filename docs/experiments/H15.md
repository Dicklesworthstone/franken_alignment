# H15 — The Manifold Premise Holds Where It Matters

- **Hypothesis ID:** `H15`
- **Founding Roots:** `FI-I02`
- **Plan Sections:** `§8.4`, `§8.5`, `§19`, `§20`
- **Preregistration Status:** `preregistered`

## Question

Are the rare, decision-relevant safety features required by oversight probes recoverable from low-dimensional sidecar activation codes, or do they reside in the low-variance residual that standard reconstruction-error objectives discard? A positive outcome establishes that the declared sidecar compression ladder preserves decision margins on the registered holdout strata; it does not prove universal feature recovery across unregistered tasks or unpinned model families.

## Treatments

1. **Raw Activation Baseline (`T0_raw`):** Uncompressed fp16/bf16 activation tensors at each declared layer and tap.
2. **Linear Subspace Projection (`T1_linear`):** Principal-subspace projections across the declared compression-ratio ladder (`2x`, `4x`, `8x`, `16x`, `32x`).
3. **Uniform / Group Quantization (`T2_quant`):** Symmetric per-head / per-group int8 and int4 quantization at matched byte budgets.
4. **Reconstruction-Trained Learned Sidecar (`T3_recon_autoencoder`):** Learned sidecar codec trained purely for mean-squared reconstruction error at each compression rung.
5. **Decision-Sensitive + Residual-Escape Sidecar (`T4_decision_residual`):** Learned sidecar codec trained with probe-margin preservation plus bounded exact-residual escape (`FA-INV-019`).

## Outcomes

- **Primary Outcome:** Per registered safety probe and layer/tap, the maximum compression ratio $R_{\text{margin}}$ at which the probe's signed decision margin on held-out harmful versus benign examples remains within $\epsilon_{\text{margin}} = 0.05$ of `T0_raw` without sign flips, compared against the maximum compression ratio $R_{\text{mse}}$ at which normalized reconstruction MSE remains below $\epsilon_{\text{mse}} = 0.05$.
- **Unconditional Denominator:** All held-out trials across both benign and harmful strata ($N_{\text{total}}$).
- **Attack-Conditioned Denominator:** All held-out elicited harmful-mechanism trials ($N_{\text{attack}}$).
- **Countermetrics:**
  - False-negative margin collapse rate on `N_attack` at each compression rung.
  - False-positive hold rate on benign strata at matched threshold.
  - Total sidecar wire bytes, exact-residual escape activation rate, cold/warm codec memory footprint, and p99 encode/decode latency.

## Controls

- **Random-Feature Control:** Random orthogonal subspace projections of identical dimension at each rung.
- **Shuffled-Label Probe Control:** Probe weights fit on permuted safety labels to verify zero spurious margin retention.
- **Both-State Evaluation:** Every probe is evaluated on both raw (`T0_raw`) and sidecar-reconstructed (`T1`–`T4`) activations on the exact same token positions.
- **Model-Revision Holdout:** Evaluation includes a held-out checkpoint revision not seen during codec or subspace fitting.

## Sample Size

- Minimum $N_{\text{attack}} \ge 500$ held-out harmful-mechanism sequences and $N_{\text{benign}} \ge 1,500$ matched benign sequences per evaluated tap ($N_{\text{total}} \ge 2,000$ per tap), partitioned across at least 4 distinct mechanism strata (`M1`–`M4`).
- No early stopping for positive results; all ladder rungs (`2x`–`32x`) must be evaluated in full.

## Confidence Method

- Stratified nonparametric bootstrap (10,000 resamples stratified by mechanism family and prompt stratum) reporting two-sided 95% confidence intervals for $R_{\text{margin}} / R_{\text{mse}}$, sign-flip rates, and AUROC deltas against `T0_raw`.
- Holm-Bonferroni correction across the evaluated compression rungs and taps.
- Any trial encountering numeric overflow, NaN, or codec budget refusal is counted as a failure in the denominator, never dropped.

## Kill Criterion

The hypothesis is falsified for pure reconstruction codecs (`T1_linear`, `T3_recon_autoencoder`) if at the target default ladder rung (`8x`), the lower 95% confidence bound on probe decision-margin retention drops below 90% of `T0_raw` or the sign-flip rate on `N_attack` exceeds 2.0% while average reconstruction MSE remains $\le 0.05$. Falsification forces the decision-sensitive objective plus exact-residual escape (`T4_decision_residual`) into the mandatory default profile and forbids unassisted reconstruction codecs from serving as decision inputs.
