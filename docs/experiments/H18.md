# H18 — Lead-Time Credit Improves Containment Without Trigger-Happy Helpers

- **Hypothesis ID:** `H18`
- **Founding Roots:** `FI-A08`, `FI-A13`
- **Plan Sections:** `§9.11`, `§11.4`, `§19`, `§20`
- **Preregistration Status:** `preregistered`

## Question

Does crediting confirmed early holds in the helper credibility ledger improve time-to-containment on real violations without increasing false holds beyond the declared andon budget or concentrating voting influence enough for an adaptive helper-hunting adversary to identify and evade the top helper?

## Treatments

1. **Uniform Congress Weights (`T0_uniform`):** Equal voting weight across all qualified helper cohorts without historical credibility weighting.
2. **Precision/Recall Weighting Only (`T1_pr_only`):** Credibility ledger updates weights from confirmed precision and recall without lead-time credit (`lead_time_bonus = 0`).
3. **Lead-Time Credit Uncapped (`T2_lead_uncapped`):** Credibility ledger awards lead-time credit for early holds prior to incident confirmation without per-helper/per-cohort influence caps.
4. **Lead-Time Credit + Influence Caps (`T3_lead_capped`):** Credibility ledger awards lead-time credit subject to hard per-helper and per-cohort influence caps and false-alarm penalties (`FA-INV-011`, `FA-INV-019`).

## Outcomes

- **Primary Outcomes:**
  1. **Mean/Median Lead-Time to Containment (`L_contain`):** Number of steps between violation onset and congress hold on confirmed violation episodes.
  2. **False-Hold Rate (`R_false_hold`):** Fraction of benign episodes halted by a congress hold against the declared andon budget.
  3. **Helper-Hunting Identification Accuracy (`Acc_hunt`):** Probability that an adaptive adversary observing binary congress decisions over a window of $W = 100$ episodes correctly identifies the highest-weight helper cohort.
- **Unconditional Denominator:** All campaign episodes across benign, delayed-label, and adversarial strata ($N_{\text{total}}$).
- **Attack-Conditioned Denominator:** All confirmed violation and helper-hunting adversarial episodes ($N_{\text{attack}}$).
- **Countermetrics:**
  - Effective cohort diversity (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index / inverse Simpson index of active helper weights).
  - Unresolved/censored-label debt accumulation when confirmation labels are delayed.
  - Post-evasion false-negative rate when the adversary suppresses features targeted by its top-guessed helper.

## Controls

- **Trigger-Happy Helper Control:** At least one helper in the congress is deliberately configured with an overly aggressive low threshold (firing early and often) to verify that false-alarm penalties dominate earliness bonuses.
- **Delayed and Censored Labels:** Confirmation labels arrive with stochastic delay ($\Delta \in [5, 50]$ episodes) and 20% censoring rate; no unconfirmed hold receives lead-time credit.
- **Mid-Campaign Cohort Rotation:** Helper rotation events occur mid-campaign to verify that credibility bounds and influence caps survive member replacement.

## Sample Size

- Minimum $N_{\text{campaigns}} \ge 50$ independent multi-episode campaigns per treatment arm, each comprising $M = 500$ episodes ($25,000$ episodes per arm; $100,000$ total episodes across `T0`–`T3`).

## Confidence Method

- Campaign-level block bootstrap (10,000 resamples across the 50 independent campaigns) for `L_contain`, `R_false_hold`, and `Acc_hunt` 95% confidence intervals.
- Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test across matched campaign seeds with Holm-Bonferroni adjustment.

## Kill Criterion

The hypothesis is falsified if under `T3_lead_capped`:
1. The trigger-happy control helper gains net credibility weight relative to calibrated helpers or pushes `R_false_hold` above the declared andon ceiling; OR
2. `Acc_hunt` exceeds uniform random guessing ($1 / K_{\text{cohorts}}$) by more than 0.10 at the upper 95% confidence bound; OR
3. `L_contain` does not improve over `T1_pr_only` under delayed labels. If falsified, lead-time credit is disabled in the default profile (`T1_pr_only` + tighter influence caps retained).
