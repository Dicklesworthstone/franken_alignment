# Terminal response to lifetime consistency incidents

`OversightBroker::enable_consistency_stop` installs an optional immutable supervisor policy before the first forecast or proposal. Without it, the original effect-hold behavior is unchanged. With it, the original likelihood process crossing its registered threshold, or recording lost coverage, triggers the original terminal `request_stop`. The policy does not classify deceptive intent, change a calibration table, reset the accumulator, increase an error budget or accept a caller-supplied alarm.

The same wrappers cover raw and owned-residual forecasting, ordinary and stream-message classification, prediction-budget exhaustion and explicit capture loss. Refused preflight inputs (wrong actor revision, stale sequence or invalid request ID) do not become new incidents. The first incident preserves its actual sample count, pending forecast, event domain and cause. A malformed stream observation remains unanswered, not a fabricated negative. A stop already installed by another path keeps that path's original operation identity. Failed containment retains its own error separately from any original observation refusal; explicit enforcement can retry without clearing evidence.

The original stop cancels only undispatched reservations and blocks further owned numerical advancement. Dispatched effects remain charged and unresolved until the original endpoint drain observes execution or establishes nonexecution. Local stop evidence is not an endpoint acknowledgment. This is synchronous containment, not a watchdog capable of acting while the process is blocked, crashed or unscheduled. Native allocator panics are not claimed to be preemptively contained; durable integration must maintain its existing unavailable boundary.

Six unit regressions pair successful original congress/publication with actual threshold crossings, unsent and unknown and executed outcomes, preflight refusals versus prediction exhaustion, malformed stream observations, fixed policy binding, and preservation of another stop operation. One compile-fail doctest rejects converting an incident to an endpoint receipt. The RCH invocation exited 127 before compilation because rch is unavailable.
