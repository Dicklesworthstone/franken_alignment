# Cooperative shutdown of durable domains

`FileOversight::shutdown_domain` captures an acknowledged owner registration. `FileShutdownPlan` fixes the explicit, nonempty domain roster, operation identity, attempt budget and retained-head byte budget before execution. `FileShutdownCampaign::advance` stops one supplied owner, drains it on a later call, or merely inspects it if already drained. The consumer is the supervisor coordinating terminal shutdown across several independently funded filesystem owners. No actor, helper, inference callback, effect permit or mutable domain owner is stored in the plan or campaign.

The coordinator calls the original `request_stop` and `progress_stop` methods. It does not implement another revocation, rights, consequence or endpoint algorithm. Original stop and drain records are committed in each owner's existing canonical journal. Shutdown uses the current local control/authority predecessor, not the older registration counters. The plan's operation identity must match any existing native stop. A stop under another operation is not relabelled as this campaign's acknowledgment. A fully drained repeat produces no new native journal event.

The complete registered denominator remains in every report. An unavailable member can be recorded and other members can progress in any order. Unvisited, failed and interrupted members cannot disappear or count as stopped through timeout or lease expiry. Each entered attempt is retained in that campaign instance; the interrupted marker and unsuccessful latest-visit state are installed before invoking native work. An older successful observation survives as history but does not substitute for a failed latest visit. Reports are data, not authority or an independently authenticated result.

Stopping and draining are deliberately separate. Undispatched reservations can be released by the original stop. Published effects and unresolved dispatched attempts remain charged until original endpoint reconciliation establishes their outcomes. The original endpoint fence, unresolved IDs, irrecoverable IDs, reserved units and charged units are retained in the observation. The latest observed native drain also retains every per-effect outcome and the revision where that sweep occurred. `all_observed_drained` does not mean executed effects were undone or all charges are zero. Retention-expired outcomes remain unresolved.

Membership is bound to the exact canonical directory, original scope/bootstrap and entire acknowledged history prefix. Each successful visit advances a byte-exact retained prefix. Different owners, older histories and equal-length forks cannot silently replace that prefix. The original journal encoder checks capacity, and the campaign checks the aggregate size of the prospective retained heads before issuing its mutation. Bounds cover attempt count and serialized current-head bytes, not RSS, wall-clock time, numerical replay cost or all report allocations. The separately retained immutable registration anchors are admitted to the same size ceiling at plan construction; anchors and newer heads can coexist.

The reported dispatch set is explicitly **since that domain's registration**. This system does not manufacture a globally ordered fleet issue point from unrelated per-domain clocks. Every drain tick belongs to its independently registered clock domain. The observations are separate local cuts, not a simultaneous distributed snapshot. This is a companion operational shutdown path to the process-local FA-120 fleet model, not a distributed implementation of its logical-order scheduler, a lease protocol or an independently running watchdog. Operators must retain the plan separately; this change does not persist a second campaign ledger.

Six integration regressions exercise multiple real files through full-input congress and separate human approval, actual publication, unresolved dispatch, unsent reservation, native stop and native drain. They cover unreachable domains with healthy controls, recovery owners, conflicting stops, exact drained repeats, registration/attempt/head limits, and expired endpoint retention. Two compile-fail doctests reject campaign cloning and report-to-permit conversion. Existing assertions, dependencies and journal encodings are unchanged.

The required RCH verification command was attempted but could not start because `rch` is unavailable (exit 127). Compilation, rustfmt, Clippy, tests and doctests remain unexecuted. The implementation does not close FA-120 or any Beads/production qualification gate. It assumes operator-controlled filesystem provenance and independently retained registrations; it establishes no cross-host authentication, physical power-loss guarantee or model-safety result.
