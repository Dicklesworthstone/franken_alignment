# Owned-model measurement through the original identity gate

`begin_hosted_learned_identity_check` connects the actual owned learned decoder
probe to the original mandatory identity challenge, observer and authority
installation (FA-118, plan section 7.10). It does not introduce an identity
verdict, second reducer or effect key.

The driver consumes the separately provisioned `IdentityObserver`. Its passport
comes from the original gate's challenge; its model comes only from the owned
learned generator. Manifest commitments are still an independently supplied
trusted host observation, NOT digests computed or authenticated by this API.
The original numerical probe computes every anchor from its registered tokens
and fresh private KV state under a whole-roster product allowance.

Each revision-checked advance performs one original anchor token, or installs
the completed original comparison. All matching anchors must complete. A real
manifest or anchor mismatch latches in the existing shared gate immediately;
the next advance applies the original suspension/revocation operation. A complete
match likewise yields before installation. Neither a measurement nor matching
identity supplies congress approval or the separate human publication key.

The cursor pins the complete learned inspection and challenge's original
control sequence, authority epoch and identity basis. Changing the actor, doing
a held numerical attempt, resetting, withdrawing identity or changing authority
terminates the cursor; it never silently adopts a newer source or predecessor.
Foreign owners, stale cursor revisions and backward clocks are free refusals.
Timeout, failed comparison, cancellation and post-admission setup errors retain
completed work and leave identity non-live. A recorded mismatch cannot be cleared
by cancellation. The same observer can be taken back only after termination.
Dropping unfinished work destroys its observer and private probe; its original
pending gate is not made live or automatically repaired.

An unreturned numerical call leaves a failed cursor and an interruption marker;
last-returned costs can understate that call, so the full planned allowance also
remains visible. Individual token computation is synchronous. Audit work is
separate from live generation work, and cancellation never refunds it. No live
KV, sampler, monitored-generation status, original numerical algorithm, or
publication policy is changed. A matching diagnostic on a held owner leaves it
held. An identity fence is not an endpoint receipt or proof of nonexecution.

Seven regression functions use original inference and authority methods. They
cover same-label changed model parameters, complete-roster progress, an explicit
pre-installation yield, fresh two-key publication, unsent versus already-executed
charges, cancellation and observer reuse, stale/foreign calls, source change,
expiry, wrong observers, insufficient budgets, repeated measurement sequences,
and diagnostic matching during a genuine hold. The existing fixtures and tests
remain unchanged. Tiny fixed intervals and supplied congress ballots are causal
controls, not discriminatory-strength or independent-evaluation evidence.

Compilation, tests, rustfmt and Clippy are UNEXECUTED: the required remote-only
RCH launcher is absent. Source and hash checks are not runtime qualification.
This in-memory integration does not journal native identity measurements, run a
randomized audit scheduler, attest a hostile host, or qualify a production model.
