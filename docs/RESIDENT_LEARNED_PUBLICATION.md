# Resident learned publication sequence

`supervise_publication serve-learned-generated` serves a finite, independently
selected sequence of learned Messages and an optional final Finish through one
original supervised owner. It keeps the authority journal locked, retains the
same admitted numerical generation, forecast observer and topology source, and
requires fresh actor ingress, native or process review and independent human
approval for each release.

An explicit `--operator-profile OPERATOR_PROFILE --tower-generation GENERATION`
selection exposes the [original live owner through Tower](LIVE_LEARNED_OPERATOR.md).
One authenticated service and its native disclosure custodians survive all
message handoffs and reconnects; spent read and transport allowances are never
renewed. Every scheduled actor/reviewer/Stop path and retained output is checked
for collision before startup. Operator traffic cannot approve or advance a
release, and independent Stop retains priority.

## Independent evaluation and Andon accounting

Fresh resident creation can select one separately authenticated evaluator and
the original false-stop accounting profile:

```text
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG SEQUENCE_PROFILE REVIEWER_PROFILE \
  --andon ANDON_PROFILE --evaluator-profile EVALUATOR_PROFILE \
  --retain-history-anchor /absolute/independent/new-resident-anchor
```

Both profiles and a new independent history output are required together. The
original atomic learned constructor installs the complete numerical recipe,
guards, native evaluation, Andon windows and terminal recovery reserve in its
first acknowledged image. No provisional unaccounted owner is created.

The accounting profile may be marginal `fa.native-andon-operator/1` or explicit
joint `fa.native-andon-operator/2`. Version two installs its complete original
`JointPromotionPolicy` in that same first image and retains it across every
Message/Finish handoff. A suffix recovery requires the independently selected
exact policy, including thresholds and replay-work ceilings. Selecting version
one does not downgrade a joint history; omitting or changing the required
policy refuses before the recovery Fence.

This is joint qualification for the original live evaluation ledger. The
separate recipe-owned held-out publication policy remains unsupported in this
accounted composition. The existing independently selected
[credibility-promotion operator](LIVE_CREDIBILITY_PROMOTION.md) requires its
explicit numerical replay allowance and native joint qualification on this
same owner. Selecting that grant with a marginal-only profile refuses during
preflight. Its one entered mutation and cumulative query/replay allowances
remain shared across all entries and operator connections. A weight transition
withdraws old identity and helper/human approvals; later publication requires
fresh original identity, source, review and human authority. See the
[joint learned operator example](LIVE_CREDIBILITY_PROMOTION.md#select-a-jointly-evaluated-learned-service)
for the paid grant and complete receipt allowance. Independent labels still
supply no publication key.

One evaluator role, listener and original finite time/candidate/I/O/byte allowance
remain attached to that owner through Message and Finish handoffs. Successful
intermediate requests service the evaluator during their bounded actor-reply and
helper-retirement windows. The original invocation lifetime includes generation
and review; no message boundary renews it. Each release still requires its exact
source, original native or process helper review and separate human approval.

After the final request, an early Hold or human rejection, remaining evaluation
time can discover and assess the already completed cases. This phase performs
no new generation, source acquisition, helper review or publication. The final
request's accepted deadline also bounds assessment. A late actor transport
failure keeps its original error and ends succession while the healthy original
evaluator retains its independent remaining opportunity. Stop and operator Fence
precede evaluation; control failure never becomes permission to keep labeling.
Evaluator authority ends before terminal reply-only grace and retained-history
capture. The final anchor therefore includes acknowledged late labels and costs.

The evaluated service also accepts an independently anchored restart:

```text
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG REMAINING_SEQUENCE REVIEWER_PROFILE \
  --after-message PREVIOUS_REQUEST --history-anchor RETAINED_INPUT \
  --andon CURRENT_ANDON_PROFILE --evaluator-profile EVALUATOR_PROFILE \
  --retain-history-anchor NEW_OUTPUT
```

Use `--continue-generation` for interrupted generation before the first request,
or `--after-message` with the original confirmed Message for a remaining Message
or Finish. A suffix may contain a single release. Supply the complete original
and current Andon window inventories and the minimum evaluation revision.
The native cursor checks the independent anchor, numerical recipe and guards,
services Stop and the deadline before open and at each replay boundary, and
acknowledges one recovery fence before it issues fresh roles. The same owner
serves every remaining release. Its finite generation budget and pending cases
survive the restart. The new evaluator can assess historical and final cases
within its single invocation allowance.

Creation and recovery admit the existing optional operator/generation
pair and per-entry version-two prechecks. Plain and simple checked publication
retain their existing guards. All scheduled role/socket/profile/recipe paths
are checked before numerical assets are loaded, including future entry paths;
recursive assets and delayed witness, identity and credential inputs cannot
overlap endpoints, authority storage or the retained output. An archive or
socket name must also leave the directory tree for a future witness available.
Governor, campaign, generation-checkpoint, predictor, held-out publication
and offline credibility-activation selections remain outside this grammar.

Use the original [evaluator-case and evaluator-assess commands](LIVE_ANDON_EVALUATION.md)
against that same endpoint. An independent label supplies no effect key and
cannot release a held message. Required RCH compilation and runtime qualification
remain pending; source review and parser checks are separate evidence.

### Retain topology through a resident evaluated sequence

Select the topology once for the whole sequence. The actor profiles and release
order come from `SEQUENCE_PROFILE`. For creation, select a
`fa.mediated-create/2` profile with the topology and inventory paths and a new
`retain_checkpoint` destination.

```sh
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG SEQUENCE_PROFILE REVIEWER_PROFILE \
  --topology-profile CREATE_TOPOLOGY \
  --andon ANDON_PROFILE --evaluator-profile EVALUATOR_PROFILE \
  --retain-history-anchor /absolute/independent/first-history.anchor
```

The first acknowledged image installs the graph, generation and selected
accounting policy together. Every entry uses the same source observer, driver
and evaluator. A later Message or Finish requires a confirmed predecessor,
fresh source checks, its own helper review and human approval. Each source
check spends the same finite allowance. A handoff cannot replenish it.
After the last assessment, the source checkpoint and history anchor describe
the same acknowledged owner, including late labels and cumulative source work.

To resume after a confirmed Message, identify that request and supply the
remaining sequence. Use a `fa.mediated-submit/2` profile with the retained
`source_checkpoint`, the next topology and inventory, and a new
`retain_checkpoint` destination.

```sh
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG REMAINING_SEQUENCE REVIEWER_PROFILE \
  --after-message PREVIOUS_REQUEST --history-anchor /absolute/independent/first-history.anchor \
  --topology-profile NEXT_TOPOLOGY \
  --andon CURRENT_ANDON_PROFILE --evaluator-profile EVALUATOR_PROFILE \
  --retain-history-anchor /absolute/independent/next-history.anchor
```

For interrupted generation, use `--continue-generation` in place of
`--after-message PREVIOUS_REQUEST`. For a sole remaining Finish, keep the
confirmed predecessor and put the Finish in the sequence profile. The source
checkpoint's embedded anchor must equal the independent input anchor and
cover the selected journal through its last event. Recovery checks the
original guards, accounting windows, evaluation floor and any joint policy
before issuing roles. It captures the recovery Fence before binding the
fresh source. If that binding fails, the two selected outputs preserve the
acknowledged recovery point. Choose separate new output paths outside the
store and clear of every entry's inputs and endpoints, including future
actor, reviewer and precheck paths.

See [topology profiles and recovery custody](MEDIATED_LEARNED_ANDON.md#use-the-live-learned-service).

## Other resident governance selections

A new nonpredictive sequence with at least two Messages can also
select `--governor-profile GOVERNOR_PROFILE` together with `--campaign-profile`
and that same operator pair. The original first-image governor remains separate
from actor, helper and human effect-review custody. Independent numerical
allowances pay for campaign Request/Approve and operator Promote; one original
service retains those allowances across every message and helper handoff. See
[live learned governance](LIVE_POLICY_GOVERNOR.md#govern-a-new-resident-learned-sequence)
for complete profiles, mode exclusions and the stable post-generation cut.

Every entry may also select the complete original joint credibility envelope.
Its policy and both work ceilings must agree across the decoded documents before
model/helper assets are loaded. The original first image installs the guard and
retains its baseline committee across independent policy promotion; it does not
activate held-out qualification. Fresh creation still refuses an activation
capsule. See [joint governed creation](LEARNED_JOINT_CREDIBILITY.md#governed-resident-creation).

This selection composes with the original `--topology-profile` creation and
recovery paths. The same constructor or guarded cursor returns both original
governor and source-observer custody. A mediated recovery requires an exact v2
source checkpoint matching the independently selected history anchor; manual
source minima cannot replace it. Source work remains cumulative, and promotion
does not replace initial certification, final fresh topology checks or human
approval. Paired source/history retention still covers returned startup errors.

An already governed stream can select the same independent governor during
explicit `--continue-generation` or `--after-message` recovery, with a mandatory
independent `--history-anchor` and full current-policy/campaign expectations in
every recipe. A recovered suffix may contain a single remaining Message or
Finish, or Messages followed by an optional Finish. A singleton Finish requires
`--after-message` naming its original confirmed predecessor and fresh review;
it cannot select a generation checkpoint or restart sampling. The original
finite generation and campaign budgets remain unchanged. The same
guarded cursor returns fresh custody after its recovery fence, while independent
Stop remains available throughout replay. See
[governed learned recovery](LIVE_POLICY_GOVERNOR.md#recover-a-governed-learned-sequence).

That explicit nonpredictive recovery can also retain an original held-out joint
guard and select one fresh `--credibility-activation CAPSULE`. The complete joint
policy remains part of every independently supplied generation, alongside the
full effective-policy and campaign requirements. The original guarded cursor
returns both roles; the governor grants no qualification or human effect key.
See [joint governed recovery](LEARNED_JOINT_CREDIBILITY.md#governed-resident-recovery).

This is scoped implementation toward FA-027 and plan sections 8.5, 8.7, 9.3,
9.11, 11.2 and 16.4. Compilation and execution qualification remain pending.
The full roadmap packet, including long-horizon and isolated-branch acceptance,
remains open.

## Command and selected entries

From a qualified Linux build:

```text
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG SEQUENCE_PROFILE REVIEWER_PROFILE
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG SEQUENCE_PROFILE REVIEWER_PROFILE --continue-generation
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG SEQUENCE_PROFILE REVIEWER_PROFILE --after-message PREVIOUS_REQUEST
```

The optional invocation-wide selections are `--topology-profile PATH`,
`--history-anchor INPUT`, `--retain-history-anchor NEW_OUTPUT`,
`--campaign-profile PATH` and `--checkpoint-before-generation PROFILE`. Explicit
live recovery may additionally select `--credibility-activation CAPSULE`
under the existing qualification contract. Recovery selection applies once,
before entry one. Creation requires a fresh authority store; existing state
must pass the original exact recovery requirements and predecessor checks.

```json
{
  "schema": "fa.learned-publication-sequence/1",
  "entries": [
    {
      "recipe": "/operator/message-1.json",
      "actor_profile": "/operator/actor-1.json",
      "release": "message"
    },
    {
      "recipe": "/operator/message-2.json",
      "actor_profile": "/operator/actor-2.json",
      "release": "message"
    },
    {
      "recipe": "/operator/finish-3.json",
      "actor_profile": "/operator/actor-3.json",
      "release": "finish"
    }
  ]
}
```

The manifest contains 2 through 16 entries for creation and unanchored sequences.
Explicit recovery with an independently selected `--history-anchor` permits
1 through 16 remaining entries, with or without a governor. Governed recovery
also retains its complete independent policy/campaign
[requirements](LIVE_POLICY_GOVERNOR.md#recover-a-governed-learned-sequence).
Each selects its own existing
[learned publication recipe](LEARNED_OPERATOR_PUBLICATION.md) and mandatory
[authenticated actor profile](LIVE_LEARNED_ACTOR.md). Recipes must admit finite
continuation, as supported by versions 4, 5 and 6. The first entry is a Message,
except for a sole remaining Finish under explicit anchored `--after-message`
recovery. Finish is optional and can appear only once, last. Ending after a
Message retains an open confirmed stream; it does not imply Finish.

A singleton Message can resume the original admitted generation or follow its
exact confirmed predecessor. A singleton Finish must name that predecessor with
`--after-message`; `--continue-generation` cannot infer it. Finish also rejects
`--checkpoint-before-generation` before recipe/model loading. A missing anchor
does not relax the original two-entry minimum. These selections preserve the
original finite numerical allowance, cumulative charges and stream prefix.
Each remaining release still needs fresh actor ingress, native/process review
and independent human approval; an Unknown predecessor cannot authorize Finish.

Every recipe must encode the exact same complete original generation and
stream. The comparison uses the original encoded configuration bytes. Prompt,
model, tokenizer, monitor, predictor and finite allowances cannot change at a
message boundary. Later equal immutable generation configurations share the
first admitted configuration; each helper roster retains its own selected
review configuration.

Request IDs, sidecar object IDs and native/process round IDs must be distinct
and nonzero. Each actor profile must select its recipe's exact request and
complete supervisor audience. Actor sockets must be distinct and cannot overlap
any selected human-review or Stop endpoint. Each entry selects exactly one
original native or process review schedule. The independent reviewer profile
and kernel credential checks remain mandatory.

Only the first entry can select recovery floors, the history anchor, topology
custody or checkpoint outputs. Secondary entries with those selections refuse
before startup. Unknown fields, missing actor profiles, changed generation,
duplicate identities and misplaced Finish also refuse during admission.

## Exact prechecks for independently selected releases

Select `fa.learned-publication-sequence/2` to choose an exact-precheck profile
for each entry. Every version-two entry must contain `precheck_profile` with
either an absolute normalized profile path or explicit `null`:

```json
{
  "schema": "fa.learned-publication-sequence/2",
  "entries": [
    {
      "recipe": "/operator/message-1.json",
      "actor_profile": "/operator/actor-1.json",
      "release": "message",
      "precheck_profile": "/operator/precheck-1.json"
    },
    {
      "recipe": "/operator/message-2.json",
      "actor_profile": "/operator/actor-2.json",
      "release": "message",
      "precheck_profile": null
    },
    {
      "recipe": "/operator/finish-3.json",
      "actor_profile": "/operator/actor-3.json",
      "release": "finish",
      "precheck_profile": "/operator/precheck-3.json"
    }
  ]
}
```

The command is unchanged. Each selected document must use the existing uncached
`fa.actor-precheck-service/1` schema with `policy_disclosure: "exact_predicates"`;
cached and full-policy disclosure profiles refuse. The
[learned release precheck contract](LIVE_ACTOR_PRECHECK.md#learned-actor-releases)
defines its request encoding, public actor client, source capture and result.
Version one remains closed and rejects the new field. Explicit null selects no
disclosure for that release and cannot inherit a predecessor's grant.

Admission loads every selected profile and checks all future endpoint names
before the first clock observation, original owner or listener. Canonical
precheck paths must be distinct from one another, all selected actor, human-review
and Stop paths, the policy source, the entire authority-store subtree, retained
history output, topology checkpoint output and the optional operator endpoint.
They must also remain distinct from every selected topology graph, inventory
and recovery source checkpoint, including future files that do not exist yet.
Canonical existing parent directories resolve symlink aliases before startup;
these name checks do not open or refresh the topology sources.
Existing files and socket paths are never replaced. The actual original policy
epoch is checked again after owner qualification and before that entry performs
new numerical generation.

The selected profiles share the same invocation-wide admission ceilings as an
ordinary precheck schedule: at most 128 queries, 128 exchanges, 1,024 candidate
connections and 1,024 authenticated connections in total. Checked sums and
distinct disclosure IDs are required before any clock, listener or recovery
Fence. Each row still owns its separate finite grant; unused work cannot move
between rows. Null rows contribute no allowance, and a sequence with no
selected disclosure retains its original admission behavior.

Each release creates its own explicitly bounded, request-bound precheck grant
during its original admission phase. Reconnecting to that endpoint does not
replenish it, and it retires before helper/human review or the next release.
Selecting a later grant does not replenish generation, source, predictor,
publication or invocation-wide operator allowances.

A pass evaluates the actual cumulative generated action for that Message or
Finish. It acquires no actor ticket or publication permission. Submit still
reacquires source evidence and requires that release's own helper congress and
human approval. Changed evidence can deny a previously passing release, and
human rejection can cancel it. Either ends the sequence before any successor;
prior confirmed effects and their charged units remain recorded.

## Functional creation, campaign guards and the first generation checkpoint

Fresh resident creation now accepts an explicitly declared `functional_restart`
CONFIG through the same scoped reader as single learned creation. Legacy
`audit_only` creation remains available. No grade is inferred or upgraded, and
`bit_exact` remains unsupported. The original first-image constructor binds the
actual numerical model, cache and sampler; an advanced supplied actor state
cannot substitute for that bootstrap.

An invocation can install the original campaign guard before any generation:

```text
supervise_publication serve-learned-generated CONFIG SEQUENCE_PROFILE REVIEWER_PROFILE \
  --campaign-profile /operator/campaign.json \
  --checkpoint-before-generation /operator/first-checkpoint.json \
  --retain-history-anchor /operator/final.anchor
```

The [campaign profile](LEARNED_CAMPAIGN_CREATION.md) selects the existing bounded
case, input-byte and campaign inventory. All independently supplied recipe
inventories must agree before this creation selection is applied. Only entry one
holds the first-image creation selection; subsequent entries retain its exact
expected inventory without installing another guard or acquiring governor
authority. Every release still needs its own native or process review and
independent human permit. The original numerical, predictor, terminal-recovery
and publication allowances are not replenished.

`--campaign-profile` is creation-only. A recovery invocation must select the
exact existing inventory in every recipe's
`fa.learned-recovery-policy/2` envelope. Recovery-only expectations cannot create
campaign resources, and a later recipe cannot replace, omit or broaden an
existing requirement.

`--checkpoint-before-generation` selects one fresh checkpoint ID and exact
position/draw count using the existing
[live checkpoint profile](LEARNED_STREAM_RESET_OPERATOR.md). It applies only to
the first Message served by this invocation. A fresh run can capture the complete
prompt before its first draw; an explicitly continued plain stream can select
its first new quiet boundary after the confirmed predecessor. This initial live
capture selection requires a plain nonpredictive stream or the explicit
[owned prompt-audit stream recipe](LEARNED_PREDICTIVE_STREAM_RESET.md), whose
capture is restricted to the complete original prompt with zero cumulative
sampling. Required raw or legacy owned predictors and topology-backed live
capture retain their refusals.

After durably capturing that boundary, the original generation loop services
Stop, source and deadline checks before the next sampled intent. Later Messages
and Finish do not capture again and cannot reuse the first checkpoint ID. A
wrong boundary refuses before sampling instead of waiting for a later matching
position. Selecting a capture grants neither reset authority nor a review key.
The checkpoint retains its original audience: once a later message is confirmed,
it cannot be used to rewind across that externally released prefix. Capturing a
checkpoint also retains the original synchronous replay/serialization cost; this
option does not introduce a new preemption or latency guarantee.

The manifest is bounded to 64 KiB. All entries and their original independently
selected assets are admitted before the first clock observation, socket bind
or authority-store startup. Each recipe retains its existing 64 MiB aggregate
asset limit, so the sequence admits at most 1 GiB of recipe assets plus bounded
profile documents. Synchronous file intake is not a preemptible service phase.

## One original owner across message boundaries

Entries may select the
[joint learned qualification envelope](LEARNED_JOINT_CREDIBILITY.md) around their
original recipes. The exact joint policy and both work ceilings must match across
every entry, alongside the existing numerical, source and predictor contracts.
The outer command now forwards `--credibility-activation` for explicit resident
recovery; fresh resident creation still refuses it before startup. The selected
native guard checks the actual capped helper weights against the original
baseline before any successful requalification can admit further work.

With a live governor selected during anchored recovery, the capsule targets the
actual recovered sequence and authority epoch, including any original topology
source rebind. It runs once before the first remaining entry's source
certification and new numerical generation. Later entries reuse the same live
qualification and check currentness without rereading the capsule. Governor Request/Approve
cannot replace it, and a policy Promote invalidates qualification for the old
policy. The next boundary requiring it refuses through the existing failure/Stop
handling; no later entry implicitly requalifies. Governed learned creation can
select the original baseline guard but still refuses activation; policy-bound
raw/owned forecast selections keep their existing refusals. The
capsule path cannot alias the governor listener, even through a canonical parent
or when the final file has not yet been provisioned.

The outer [computed-identity envelope](LEARNED_IDENTITY_OPERATOR.md) can require
a fresh original-model measurement for every entry. All entries retain the same
complete passport and policy, distinct check IDs, strictly increasing measurement
sequences and the original check allowance. These requirements are admitted before
runtime startup. The invocation retains its unique observer and completed or
cancelled run history; expired or interrupted measurements cannot admit publication.

An outer [checked-publication envelope](LEARNED_CHECKED_PUBLICATION.md) can require
the original structured or opaque publication witnesses for every entry. All
entries retain the same exact native validation, feed, freshness and snapshot
policy. Each actual Message and Finish binds its own original action and evidence
before review, then reacquires current evidence during review and at the original
authorization, dispatch and publication boundaries. Per-attempt source paths and
witness requests may differ. A failed later basis preserves the prior confirmed
message and its charge; no prior capture, review or human key supplies the next
entry's permission.

The invocation creates or recovers one original host and releases one actor
gateway, driver, human reviewer and, when required, forecast observer. Its
topology custodian stays attached through numerical preparation, native or
process congress and final publication. Intermediate messages do not reopen
the authority journal or introduce a recovery Fence or fresh qualification.

For every entry, the ordinary learned request cycle still:

1. Checks the current source, deadline and authenticated Stop service.
2. Completes the original required forecast before sampling, or the separate
   forecast required for Finish.
3. Prepares the admitted original numerical continuation and accepts the
   selected authenticated actor release with its own deadline.
4. Runs the selected native or process review schedule and obtains a fresh
   independent human decision for that exact action.
5. Publishes only through the original driver and reports its original result.

The next actor listener is bound only after the predecessor has both an
original `Known(Executed)` response and a native `Confirmed` request with an
`Executed` resolution. The driver must retire its original direct children;
separately launched process reviewers must also be confirmed reaped. Their
histories remain owned through cleanup. Ambiguous or still-running work cannot
supply a successor slot.

Required forecasts retain their original consumed record and observer. Raw
forecasts and owned learned forecasts use their own native observation
accessors. Numerical draws, forecast expenditure, source checks, authority
charges and the admitted stream prefix accumulate across the sequence.
Individual request/helper quotas cannot refill those original lifetime
allowances.

This removes reopening and guarded authority-history recovery between entries.
The original cooperative numerical preparation still performs whatever replay
its existing stepper requires. The command does not admit arbitrary new prompts,
an unbounded conversation, branch credentials or replacement model assets.

## Deadlines, review and Stop

One global logical deadline and one monotonic wall deadline span the live
sequence. The runtime is bounded by CONFIG and every selected actor runtime.
Each request is additionally limited by its recipe TTL and actor runtime;
the accepted actor deadline can only tighten its action deadline. The first
entry's deadline includes startup and any initial replay. A later short TTL
retains a wall-time bound even if the logical clock stops advancing.

For each request, use the original `review-peer REVIEWER_PROFILE REQUEST`
client when its fresh review socket is announced. It displays the original
offer and requires the exact explicit decision and session nonce. Selecting a
sequence does not preapprove any human decision or submit any actor request.

The invocation retains one authenticated Stop endpoint and operation, named by
the **first recipe's request ID**, throughout every entry. If that ID is 1:

```text
supervise_publication stop-peer /operator/reviewer.json 1
```

The same operation can be selected in a [live fleet Stop roster](LIVE_FLEET_STOP.md).
Later request IDs never replace it. Operational failure during a later entry
also uses this original Stop operation and the existing reserved drain path.

Cancellation or another request-local nonexecuted result ends the sequence
without starting the next request. Unknown dispatches keep their original
charged liability. Earlier confirmed publications remain facts. Failed cleanup,
source retention, output or a lost acknowledgement cannot trigger an effect
retry or restore capacity.

### An explicitly selected successor after Hold

A resident sequence may select the operator profile's finite `convening`
grant when each scheduled recipe retains exactly one original native or process
evaluator owner. Ordinary recipes and exact required raw or owned pre-output
predictors can use the grant. Each predictive Message or separately forecast
Finish retains its own settled observation, coverage and lifetime work; a prior
release cannot supply another request's evidence or renew its predictor
allowance.

The invocation reserves every scheduled request's fixed review IDs before
issuing its one operator grant. An explicit continuation cannot take a future
release's native or process round ID. The current request can select only its
own offered next fixed slot, or an unused ID for its separately funded native
reserve.

Progressive native recipes retain their original round IDs, evaluator rosters
and windows. Process recipes likewise declare every executable roster and
window before the first review; actionable process continuation requires
multiple fixed slots and their original start, sidecar and poll capacity.
Confirmed reaping of every predecessor direct child is required before taking
a process ticket or acquiring the next policy/topology observation.

A one-round **native** recipe can instead preprovision additional complete
helper rosters before Begin. The service reserves the largest fully funded
count within that recipe's original native work limits, the shared grant's
remaining operations and 32 total initial-plus-reserved rounds. Zero funded
successors remain accepted without actionable continuation. A single-round
process recipe cannot use this native reserve or manufacture another roster.

After an applied Hold without refinement, the service retains that request's
original wrapper for authenticated explicit Next/Convene. Either fixed schedule
opens only its exact next preprovisioned round within the original fixed
window. A funded native reserve accepts an unused round ID and a host-selected
window within the original action deadline. Every cohort was fixed before the
initial Begin.

Waiting retains the same run-wide Stop endpoint, actor cancellation, operator
transport counters and logical/wall deadlines. It does not acquire policy
evidence, spend a native evaluation/process protocol poll, or regenerate actor
output. Process child cleanup remains active. A process continuation launch
spends one original protocol poll; later review quanta share the original
cumulative allowance. Expiry or grant revocation ends the wait. A consumed
source or launch failure retires remaining custody without refund.

A new Continue still requires an independent human decision and the original
guarded publication. The next resident release remains blocked until the
current request is confirmed Executed and its original helper cleanup completes.
Operator operations and queries remain spent across releases and reconnects;
a final already admitted Pending ticket can finish without replenishing the
grant. Live recovered suffixes can continue newly admitted requests that reach
a fresh Hold, while historical held receipts cannot recreate an evaluator owner.

See [native held review](LEARNED_HELD_REVIEW.md),
[fixed process held review](PROCESS_LEARNED_HELD_REVIEW.md),
[one-round native reserves](LEARNED_RESERVED_REVIEW.md) and
[live learned operator selection](LIVE_LEARNED_OPERATOR.md) for the complete
custody and source contracts. Regression source is authored and reviewed;
required RCH compilation and runtime qualification remain pending.

## Retained evidence and results

Topology input and output use the existing
[mediated creation and continuation profiles](TOPOLOGY_CHECKPOINT_OPERATOR.md).
A mediated recovery requires the independently retained history anchor and its
matching source checkpoint. The invocation retains the selected final topology
checkpoint and history anchor exactly once, while the original driver still
owns its final acknowledged state. They cover all completed entries and
preserve cumulative source expenditure. Output paths must be distinct, fresh
and outside the authority store under their existing custody rules.

A refusal may still leave a healthy source whose final checkpoint can be
retained. Storage or export failures remain errors; an incomplete output is
not acknowledged evidence. Preserve the original independently retained inputs.

Each original actor receives its normal response service. At invocation end,
stdout emits the original encoded responses in entry order, one per line.
Success requires every selected entry to be confirmed Executed, no operational
failure and completed cleanup. A nonzero exit or lost stdout does not establish
that no effect occurred. Inspect the original request through the existing
[receipt service](LEARNED_RECEIPT_SERVICE.md) or recovery command; do not submit
a replacement effect merely because an acknowledgement is missing.

## Verification scope

Regression source drives original imported numerical generation, authenticated
actor and human peers, native congress and separately launched process fixtures.
It covers all three forecast modes, A/A/Finish continuity, native/process/native
review, cancellation, lifetime forecast exhaustion, an actual post-Dispatch

admission, full on-disk command/topology/export composition, and a short request

missing independent actors, extra fields and early Finish.
